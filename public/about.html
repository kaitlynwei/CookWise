About CookWise

CookWise is a beginner-friendly cooking website with clear recipes from several cuisines, ranging from simple meals to more detailed dishes.

Each recipe combines practical cooking instructions with adjustable servings, approximate macros, source attribution, provider scores, difficulty, and cook time.

The Goal

CookWise aims to help home cooks try new cuisines, plan practical meals, understand basic nutrition, and build useful kitchen skills one recipe at a time.

Recipe Sources and Selection

CookWise imports real recipes from Spoonacular and links back to each credited publisher. Recipes are filtered for clear instructions, practical cook times, no more than 16 listed ingredients, provider rating, popularity, and nutrition quality.

Provider and health scores are useful ranking signals, not guarantees that a recipe is right for every person. CookWise does not claim that every recipe has been independently tested by the CookWise team.

Important Notes

  • Macro values are provider estimates and may change by brand.
  • Recipe availability and provider scores can change.
  • Nutrition information is educational, not medical advice.
  • Users should check labels when managing allergies.
