Example Recipe: Mediterranean Chickpea Bowl

Difficulty: Easy

Approximate cooking time: 25 minutes

Recommended serving size: 1 bowl per person

Ingredients for 2 people

  • 1 can chickpeas, rinsed
  • 1 cup cooked brown rice or quinoa
  • 1 cup chopped cucumber and tomato
  • 2 tablespoons hummus
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 lemon

Substitutions

  • Use white beans or lentils instead of chickpeas.
  • Use any cooked whole grain instead of brown rice.
  • Use plain yogurt or tahini instead of hummus.

Instructions

  1. Prepare 1 cup of brown rice or quinoa according to its package instructions.
  2. Rinse 1 can of chickpeas and divide it between two bowls.
  3. Add 1 cup of cooked grain and 1 cup of chopped cucumber and tomato.
  4. Top each bowl with 1 tablespoon of hummus.
  5. Drizzle the bowls with 1 tablespoon of olive oil and the juice of 1/2 lemon, then serve.

Estimated macros per serving

  • Calories: 430 per serving; 860 total
  • Protein: 16 grams per serving; 32 grams total
  • Carbohydrates: 58 grams per serving; 116 grams total
  • Fat: 16 grams per serving; 32 grams total
  • Fiber: 11 grams per serving; 22 grams total
  • Sugar: 7 grams per serving; 14 grams total
  • Sodium: 480 milligrams per serving; 960 milligrams total

Ingredients and recipe totals are shown for 2 people.

Macro Guide

Protein

Protein helps build and repair body tissue and can make meals more satisfying. Including a protein source throughout the day may be more practical than trying to consume most of it in one meal.

Current U.S. guidance gives a general target of 1.2–1.6 grams per kilogram of body weight per day. A 125-pound person is about 57 kilograms, which is approximately 68–91 grams of protein per day. These amounts are approximations.

Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates are an important source of energy. Whole grains, legumes, fruits, and vegetables also provide fiber and other useful nutrients.

Fat

Dietary fat supports cells, hormones, and the absorption of certain vitamins. Unsaturated fats from foods such as nuts, seeds, fish, and olive oil can be useful parts of balanced meals.

Meal timing and portions

There is no single meal schedule that works for everyone. Hunger, activity, health needs, and personal routines affect useful meal timing and portions. Macro estimates are approximations.

General 2,000-Calorie Daily Pattern

  • Protein foods: 3–4 servings
  • Dairy or fortified alternatives: 3 servings
  • Vegetables: 3 servings
  • Fruits: 2 servings
  • Whole grains: 2–4 servings
  • Healthy fats: about 4 1/2 teaspoons
  • Fiber: at least 28 grams
  • Sodium: less than 2,300 milligrams

These amounts are approximations.

About CookWise

CookWise is made for beginner cooks who want to prepare more meals at home without feeling overwhelmed. It gathers approachable recipes from many cuisines and presents clear information about ingredients, instructions, difficulty, cooking time, servings, and nutrition.

The purpose of CookWise is to move people from browsing to cooking. One daily recommendation offers a simple place to begin, while the full recipe collection changes order regularly so new ideas and familiar favorites both get noticed.

Personalized Support

In the full application, signed-in users can save preferences for serving count, cuisine, maximum cooking time, and minimum protein per serving. Closer matches appear near the top while CookWise continues to consider overall recipe quality.

Motivation and Progress

Users can record dishes they complete, review their recent cooking history, and stack achievements. Taste Trailblazer celebrates trying a new recipe, and Encore Expert celebrates cooking the same recipe at least four times.

Recipe Information

The full CookWise application imports recipes from Spoonacular and links to each credited original publisher. Recipes are checked for usable ingredients, instructions, practical cooking time, provider rating, and reasonable nutrition estimates. Less-than-ideal recipes may include educational guidance instead of being automatically removed.

Imported punctuation is checked automatically. Ingredient amounts use U.S. measurements, and metric temperatures or pan dimensions are converted to Fahrenheit and inches. Vague instructions show relevant measured ingredients when they can be matched accurately.

Privacy and Responsible Use

The full application stores accounts, preferences, achievements, and cooking history in a local SQLite database. Passwords are protected with salted hashes. Private API keys and database files are excluded from the public GitHub repository.

Macro values are approximations. Users managing allergies should always check labels and the original recipe source.
